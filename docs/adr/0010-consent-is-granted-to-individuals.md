# Consent is granted to individual Clinicians, not to Provider organisations

A Patient grants a named Clinician access to their history. There is no mechanism to grant access to a Provider and thereby to its staff.

## Why

Real hospital systems do organisation-level grants, and that is precisely how "I consented to my hospital seeing my records" comes to mean four thousand people can read them. A patient can meaningfully agree that *Dr Meera Nair* may read their history. Nobody can meaningfully agree that *a hospital* may — informed consent to an unbounded, changing set of people is not informed.

It also happens to be simpler: no staff-membership resolution at query time, no question of what happens to access when someone changes jobs mid-consent.

## Consequences

A Clinician moving between Providers keeps their consents, because the consent was never about the Provider. That is the correct behaviour and worth stating, since the opposite is often assumed.

Provider Staff still read entries their own Provider authored — that is a separate rule and needs no consent, or the upload portal could not function.

A patient seen by several doctors at one hospital grants several consents. Accepted: the alternative makes the grant meaningless.
